# 10. 117 of the 327 progress checkpoints in `game_progress.json` are wrong

## Where

- Hugging Face dataset [`longphann/textquests`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/longphann/textquests), revision `be34a3a`, `textquests_data.zip`: `game_progress.json` (the same file is `data/jiminy-cricket/game_progress.json` in [centerforaisafety/textquests](https://github.com/centerforaisafety/textquests) at `18dc472`).
- It is matched as a case-insensitive substring of the observation by [simple-evals `textquests/src/textquests_env.py` lines 249–254](https://github.com/centerforaisafety/simple-evals/blob/8e53435/textquests/src/textquests_env.py#L249-L254) (live) and [`textquests/textquests_eval.py` lines 282–290](https://github.com/centerforaisafety/simple-evals/blob/8e53435/textquests/textquests_eval.py#L282-L290) (offline), and a game's progress is the highest percentage matched.

## What happens

Each checkpoint is a percentage and one string, and counts as reached when the string occurs in the game's text. Each string was traced to the routine in the game's source (the `.zil` files in the data) that prints it, and the other routes to the same milestone were played. 117 of the 327 strings are wrong:

| Fault                                                                            | Checkpoints |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------: |
| False positive: the text also prints when the milestone has not been reached     | 42          |
| False negative: a legitimate way of reaching the milestone prints different text | 65          |
| Both                                                                             | 10          |

Within these:

- **The 100% text prints in a losing or lesser ending** in five games: cutthroats, planetfall, plunderedhearts, sorcerer and trinity. The harness ends a game at 100% progress, so it records these endings as finished games.
- **Seven can be scored by typing their own words.** The parser quotes the player's noun phrase back (`You can't see any bolt cutter here.`), so a bare object or room name is matched from the first move.
- **Three depend on a random value**: the text is one of several the game picks at random (trinity 30%), names something drawn at random (borderzone 93%, the password, one of four), or needs a character who wanders at random (enchanter 60%, the adventurer in the Hall of Mirrors).
- **Nine cannot be fixed by changing the text**: the game does not print anything that separates reaching the milestone from not reaching it.

Two or three worked examples of each kind follow. Each names replay cases from `tests/textquests/verified_cases.json` in this repository; "Reproduce" below runs them against upstream's environment.

### Fires on a losing ending or a wrong action

- **sorcerer 100%**, `Jeearr surrounds you like a cloud and begins to contract`. After the walkthrough's 253rd action, `get dagger` and `kill belboz with dagger` print the string, kill Belboz and end the game at 375 of 400 points. Upstream's environment reports progress 100 and the game over, and the harness stops the game as finished. The winning branch alone prints `Belboz moans softly, and begins stirring`. Cases `sorcerer/100-kill-belboz-original` (occurs) and `sorcerer/100-kill-belboz-proposed` (absent).
- **trinity 100%**, `A shower of sparks erupts from the enclosure.` The sentence prints for every wire, and for the right wire cut before the sequencer starts. Case `trinity/100-wrong-wire-original-fires`: `cut blue wire` after the walkthrough's 575th action prints it.
- **cutthroats 30%**, `Let's go get what we need`. The same sentence follows both boat choices; answering NO rents the Night Wind, which does not carry a deep-sea suit, and the game is then lost under seed 1. Case `cutthroats/30-wrong-boat-original-fires`.

### Scored by typing its own words

- **lurkinghorror 70%**, `bolt cutter`. `get bolt cutter` on the first move is answered `You can't see any bolt cutter here.` Case `lurkinghorror/70-echo-at-start-original-fires`. Taking the cutter prints only `Taken.`, so on the walkthrough the checkpoint fires later, when the cutter is used or listed.
- **zork2 65%**, `priceless gold zorkmid`. `examine priceless gold zorkmid` on the first move prints it. Case `zork2/65-echo-original-fires`.

### A correct alternative command misses it

- **sherlock 35%**, `You take the emerald out of the wax pool`. `get all` after the wax head has melted takes the emerald and scores the points, but prints `The emerald: Taken.` Cases `sherlock/35-get-all-orig-misses` (absent) and `sherlock/35-get-all-proposed` (occurs).
- **enchanter 5%**, `The scroll reads "rezrov spell`. `gnusto spell` copies the scroll into the spell book without reading it (the parser asks which spell, by name), and the string does not print. Cases `enchanter/5-fn-gnusto-unread-original` and `enchanter/5-fn-gnusto-unread-proposed`.
- **zork2 5%**, `lamp: Taken.` Only a multi-object take prints this; the first move `take lamp` prints `Taken.` Case `zork2/5-take-lamp-original-misses`.

### Depends on a random value

- **trinity 30%**, `You take the lump of metal.` This is one of three messages the game picks at random for the same take. Waiting one turn before taking the lump gives another (case `trinity/30-pick-up-original-absent`), and the replacement text covers all three (`trinity/30-pick-up-proposed`).
- **borderzone 93%**, `"Belboz"`. The string is the password the player is told, which the game draws at random; under interpreter seed 5 the same walkthrough is told `Brzni` and the checkpoint does not fire.

## Reproduce

The replay cases are in `tests/textquests/verified_cases.json` in this repository (1,034 cases). Each plays a number of walkthrough actions (or a list of actions), then the case's actions, and says whether any of its strings occurs, case-insensitively, in the cleaned observation of any of those actions. The script below runs them against upstream's environment. In a checkout of simple-evals at `8e53435`, with `jericho` and `numpy` installed and the data unpacked at `textquests/data/textquests/` (where `textquests_eval.py` unpacks it), save it as `textquests/cases.py` and run `python cases.py <path to verified_cases.json> [game or game/case ...]` from `textquests/`.

```python
import json, os, sys, tempfile
sys.path.insert(0, ".")  # run from simple-evals/textquests
from src.textquests_env import TextQuestsEnv

DATA = os.path.abspath("data/textquests")
cases = json.load(open(sys.argv[1]))
wanted = set(sys.argv[2:])  # optional: "game" or "game/case-name"
os.chdir(tempfile.mkdtemp())

failed = 0
for case in cases:
    game = case["game"]
    if wanted and game not in wanted and f"{game}/{case['name']}" not in wanted:
        continue
    env = TextQuestsEnv(f"{DATA}/{game}")
    lines = open(f"{DATA}/{game}/{game}_walkthrough.txt").read().split("\n")
    walkthrough = [line for line in lines if line.strip()]
    setup = case["setup"]
    seen = False
    for action in setup.get("actions", walkthrough[: setup.get("walkthrough_upto", 0)]):
        env.step(action)
    for action in case["actions"]:
        observation = env.step(action)[0].lower()
        seen = seen or any(s.lower() in observation for s in case["strings"])
    ok = seen == case["expect"]
    failed += not ok
    print("PASS" if ok else "FAIL", f"{game}/{case['name']}", "occurs" if seen else "absent")
print(f"{failed} failed")
```

With only the path to the cases file it runs all 1,034, and all pass against upstream's environment. A case named `...-original...` tests the string in `game_progress.json`; one named `...-proposed...` tests the replacement in the tables below.

This script finds the checkpoints that can be scored by typing their words, from the opening position of each game:

```python
import json, os, sys, tempfile
sys.path.insert(0, ".")  # run from simple-evals/textquests
from src.textquests_env import TextQuestsEnv

DATA = os.path.abspath("data/textquests")
progress = json.load(open(f"{DATA}/game_progress.json"))
os.chdir(tempfile.mkdtemp())

# From the opening position, type each checkpoint's own words after a verb
for game in sorted(progress):
    env = TextQuestsEnv(f"{DATA}/{game}")
    start = env.get_state()
    for checkpoint in progress[game]["checkpoints"]:
        text = checkpoint["observation_string_check"]
        if checkpoint["percentage"] == 0 or "\n" in text:
            continue
        for verb in ["examine", "take", "find"]:
            action = f"{verb} {text}"
            if len(action) > 64:  # longer actions are quoted back by the System Warning line
                continue
            env.set_state(start)
            obs, *_ = env.step(action)
            if text.lower() in obs.lower():
                print(f"{game:14} {checkpoint['percentage']:3}%  {action!r:36} -> {obs.strip()!r}")
                break
```

## Effect on results

- Scoring the logged observations of the two 500-step runs with the tag format by upstream's rule (highest checkpoint matched), with the replacement texts below in place of the faulty ones and the nine undecidable checkpoints left out, GPT-4.1's mean progress goes from 20.0 to 18.4 (enchanter 60 to 15, spellbreaker 0 to 5) and GPT-5's from 38.0 to 36.6 (starcross 80 to 55, enchanter 60 to 45, sherlock 0 to 5). In both runs enchanter's 60% came from the hint dream on `sleep`.
- A losing or lesser ending in cutthroats, planetfall, plunderedhearts, sorcerer or trinity is reported as a finished game at 100%.
- A model that types an object's name (for example `ask hacker about bolt cutter`) is credited with lurkinghorror's 70% on the first move.

## Relation to Epoch AI's review

[Epoch AI's review](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/textquests/review) reports 92 findings on 74 of the 327 checkpoints: 22 "impossible", 12 "correct marked wrong", 23 "incorrect marked correct" and 35 "inert".

- **Confirmed.** 50 checkpoints have a flag other than "inert". The text of 40 of them is wrong. For two of the 40 a different fault applies from the one flagged: cutthroats 90% fires on the line swinging past, not the tie, but in the replays the line could not be tied without that text printing first, so Epoch's "correct marked wrong" does not apply; zork2 5% misses `take lamp`, but the sword that Epoch's "incorrect marked correct" refers to is not needed to win (the dragon and the aquarium can be dealt with without it). cutthroats 80%, flagged "impossible", is also scored by typing its words.
- **Not confirmed as faults in the text (10).** Seven are flagged "impossible" because they need a package item the agent is not given (report 09): infidel 20%, lurkinghorror 5% and 100%, plunderedhearts 40%, and sherlock 55%, 70%, 90% and 100%. The text is correct, and sherlock's are reachable by retrying at different hours until the tide is high. trinity 95% is "impossible" only on the bundled walkthrough, which is longer than 500 steps; a 465-action route reaches it at its 444th action. lurkinghorror 75% ("incorrect marked correct") is printed only in the scoring branch and cannot be typed.
- **"Inert".** A checkpoint is inert when it fires after a higher one on the walkthrough. That is a property of the highest-checkpoint metric, not of the text, and is not counted as a fault here. 24 checkpoints carry only this flag; 8 of them are wrong for another reason.
- **Not flagged.** The review does not flag 69 of the 117 (46 false negatives, 21 false positives, 2 both), and flags 8 more only as "inert".

## Suggested fix

- Replace the 106 strings and leave out the 9 checkpoints listed below. planetfall's 10% and 40% fail only through the blacklist gap of report 01 and are correct once chained `superbrief` is refused.
- Match checkpoints on the cleaned observation without the System Warning line (report 03), so that typed words are not scored.
- Report progress as the share of checkpoints reached, as these games are not linear and the highest checkpoint says little about how much of a game was played.
